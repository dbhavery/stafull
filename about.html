About

We brought the gas station to you.

StaFull exists to delete one of the most pointless errands left in modern life: the trip to the pump. We deliver fuel where your vehicles already sit — for less than the station charges, and with a record of every gallon.

Our story

A simple idea, built right.

StaFull Holdings, LLC is a Delaware company founded by Donald B. Havery on one observation: fueling is slow, opaque, and overpriced for no good reason. Drivers detour to stations, fleets bleed money they can't trace, and everyone pays the pump's retail markup.

So we flipped it. Instead of sending people to the fuel, we bring the fuel to the people — on-site, on a schedule, by electric tanker. Because we buy wholesale and skip the storefront, we deliver below pump price and still run a clean, accountable operation. That's the whole company: effortless fueling, proven cheaper, every drop verified.

How we operate

Three systems behind every fill.

Electric delivery fleet

Route-optimized electric tankers carry diesel, gas, premium and DEF straight to driveways, lots and yards — refueling vehicles in place, in your scheduled window, with a fraction of the trips and emissions.

Franchise & territory model

StaFull scales city by city through local operators. Each franchisee runs their own electric-tanker territory on our pricing, technology and compliance playbook — so a new metro launches with a known standard, not a guess.

Safety & compliance

AI photo verification confirms the vehicle, plate and fuel before a drop flows. Behind it: trained HazMat handling, spill-response procedures, calibrated metering, and grounding and overfill interlocks on every delivery.

What we believe

Four commitments we don't bend on.

Cheaper than the pump — and prove it

Every fill is billed below your local average and shown on the receipt. Savings you can see, not a slogan.

Safety first, always

HazMat-trained crews, calibrated equipment, grounding and overfill interlocks, and spill response on standby. Fuel handled the right way, every time.

Clean electric delivery

Never another station trip

Your time is the point. We fuel where you park so the errand simply disappears — for one car or a whole fleet.
