Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Sample templated language

This policy explains what information StaFull collects when you use our delivery service and account tools, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to drivers, fleet operators, and prospective franchisees who interact with us.

We collect the account details you provide: name, contact information, billing details, vehicle make and model, license plate, fuel type, and where your vehicles are parked. To verify each fill, we capture delivery records and photographs of the vehicle and fuel port at the point of delivery, along with the gallons dispensed, grade, timestamp, and location.

We use this information to schedule and complete deliveries, confirm the correct vehicle and fuel type before dispensing, bill you accurately, provide support, and improve safety and route efficiency. Verification photos and delivery logs are retained as part of your fill history and our compliance records. We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only with the people and providers needed to run the service, for example, payment processors, and, for franchised territories, the local StaFull operator fulfilling your delivery. We may disclose information where required by law or to investigate safety incidents, fuel theft, or fraud.

You can access, correct, export, or request deletion of your information at any time, subject to records we must keep for billing, tax, safety, and legal obligations. To make a request or ask a question about your data, contact us at hello@stafull.com.

Safety & Compliance

Last updated: June 2026 · Sample templated language

Delivering fuel on-site is a regulated activity, and safety is built into every step of how StaFull operates. We transport and dispense diesel, gasoline, premium, and DEF in accordance with applicable U.S. Department of Transportation HazMat regulations and state and local fire-code requirements for mobile fueling.

Every StaFull tanker carries the placarding, secondary containment, fire extinguishers, and spill-response equipment required for its load. Our drivers complete HazMat and defensive-driving certification, are trained in emergency shut-off and spill procedures, and follow a documented pre-delivery checklist at each site before any fuel flows.

At the vehicle, our equipment uses bonding and grounding to prevent static discharge and automatic overfill-prevention interlocks that stop the flow before a tank can be overfilled. Deliveries are metered, and the nozzle is controlled by a trained operator the entire time. Fuel is never left running unattended.

Before a single drop is dispensed, the driver checks the vehicle, its fuel port, and the correct fuel type against your account and photographs the match. This is how we prevent cross-fueling, putting the wrong grade in the wrong vehicle, which protects your engine and our record of every fill.

In the event of a spill or leak, drivers follow a defined response protocol: stop the flow, contain the release with on-board absorbents and containment, secure the area, and notify the appropriate authorities and our operations team as required. Incidents are logged, reviewed, and used to improve our procedures.

StaFull maintains commercial and pollution-liability insurance appropriate to mobile fueling operations, and our franchised operators are required to carry equivalent coverage and meet the same certification and equipment standards before delivering under the StaFull name.
