The problems we kill

Everything the fuel island costs you, fixed.

Driver detours & downtime

Sending drivers to fuel islands burns paid hours and miles before a route even starts.

StaFull fixes it: our tanker comes to your lot on your schedule, so every truck starts the day full without ever leaving the yard.

Fuel-card leakage

Fuel cards leak: personal fill-ups, snacks, and off-policy purchases that never touch your trucks.

StaFull fixes it: no cards at all. Fuel goes straight into your vehicles, billed at what was actually pumped.

Trucks not ready at shift start

A half-empty truck at 6 a.m. means a driver fuels on the clock or rolls out late, and the whole route slips behind.

StaFull fixes it: your tanker comes pre-shift, so every truck is topped off and route-ready before the first driver clocks in.

Zero visibility

Spreadsheets and paper tickets hide what each vehicle actually burns and what it actually costs.

StaFull fixes it: every gallon and dollar is logged per vehicle in your account, in real time, with no reconciliation.

How fleet service works

Onboard once. Then your yard just stays full.

We map your yard & vehicles

Tell us where your fleet parks and what each unit takes. We build a per-vehicle profile (make, tank, fuel grade, and lot position) so deliveries are routed and verified down to the truck.

Our tanker refuels on-site

An electric StaFull tanker arrives in your scheduled window and fills every vehicle in place. Before fueling, the driver checks the vehicle and the fuel grade against your account.

One invoice, NET terms, full visibility

Every fill lands on a single verified invoice billed below the local pump. Pay on NET 15, 30, or 45 terms, and see every gallon and dollar broken out per vehicle in your account.
