How the savings work

You pay less than the pump, and the receipt proves it.

We buy wholesale

StaFull purchases fuel at wholesale volume and routes electric tankers efficiently, so our cost is well under the retail pump. We pass that margin straight to you, every gallon is billed below your local average.

Every fill is itemized

Each receipt shows the local pump price that night, your StaFull price, and the dollars you saved on that fill. Nothing is estimated or buried, you see exactly what the gas station would have charged.

No cards, no detours

No fuel cards to manage, no station trips, no rewards-program math. The fuel comes to where your vehicle is parked, and the savings show up automatically on every invoice.

Questions

The fine print, plainly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every personal plan is month-to-month (or year, for Annual) with no contracts and no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account and your last scheduled fill still goes through, you only pay for fuel you've received.

Which fuels do you deliver?

Diesel, regular gas, premium, and DEF. The driver checks the vehicle and the fuel type before fueling, so a diesel truck does not get gas and a gas car does not get diesel.

How does billing actually work?

Your plan fee is a flat monthly or annual charge that covers scheduling and on-site delivery. Fuel is billed separately at our below-pump rate for the gallons actually dispensed on each fill. Both appear on one itemized statement in your account.

Is it really cheaper than the pump?

Yes, and you don't have to take our word for it. Because we buy wholesale, every gallon is billed below your local pump price, and each receipt shows the pump price that night next to your StaFull price and the exact savings. The higher tiers add extra fuel savings on top.

What areas do you serve?

StaFull currently delivers across the Dallas-Fort Worth, TX metro for both residential drivers and commercial fleets. Check the coverage area to confirm your address or yard is in range.

Are there minimums for fleets?

Fleet accounts are priced by volume rather than vehicle count, so the right fit depends on your gallons and locations. Tell us about your fleet on the fleet program page or contact us for a custom quote with NET terms.
