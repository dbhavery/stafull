How franchise economics work

Franchisees run their own trucks, drivers and customer book; HQ provides the brand, the dispatch & verification platform, and national supply agreements. In return each location remits a 6% royalty on revenue, a 1% brand-fund fee that pays for national marketing, and a $600 per-truck monthly technology lease covering the tankers' telemetry, the AI fuel-type verification, and the customer apps. The Total to HQ column is the sum of those three lines — the recurring, asset-light revenue the holding company collects across the network.
